Medical Aesthetics

APTOS Thread Lift

Subtle Lifting and Tissue Support for Selected Patients

APTOS thread lifting is a minimally invasive procedure that places specialized threads beneath the skin to provide mechanical support and stimulate a healing and collagen response. It may offer a subtle lift for carefully selected patients with mild to moderate tissue descent.

Thread lifting does not replace a surgical facelift and cannot remove excess skin. Patient selection and realistic expectations are essential.

Consultation

Dina assesses facial anatomy, tissue thickness, degree of laxity, asymmetry, previous procedures and medical history. Patients with significant laxity, unrealistic expectations or certain health conditions may be better served by another option or a surgical consultation.

Medical Aesthetics

Procedure and Recovery

The procedure is performed using local anesthesia and specialized insertion techniques. Swelling, bruising, tenderness, tightness, puckering or temporary asymmetry may occur during early healing. Activity and facial-movement restrictions may be recommended for a period after treatment.

NBProcedure and Recovery image placeholder

Medical Aesthetics

Risks

Potential complications include infection, prolonged pain, palpable or visible threads, dimpling, asymmetry, thread migration or extrusion, nerve irritation, scarring and an unsatisfactory result. Some complications may require medication, thread adjustment or removal, or referral.

Helpful information

Thread Lift FAQ

Will the result look like a facelift?+

No. Thread lifting offers a more limited and less predictable degree of lifting than surgery.

How long do results last?+

Duration varies with anatomy, tissue quality, technique and the natural aging process. No fixed duration can be guaranteed.

Can threads be combined with injectables?+

Possibly. The safest sequence and timing depend on the individual plan.
