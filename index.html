About the clinic

Personalized Care for Beauty, Health and Confidence

At Natural Beauty Medical Clinic, aesthetic care begins with understanding you—your features, your concerns and the way you would like to feel.

Every treatment plan is personalized following a comprehensive consultation. Rather than focusing on a single line or isolated area, Dina considers facial anatomy, proportion, movement, skin quality and your long-term goals. The result is a balanced approach that enhances your natural beauty while preserving expression and individuality.

Whether you are interested in softening expression lines, restoring facial volume, navigating perimenopause or menopause, managing your weight or supporting your overall wellness, your care is guided by clinical assessment, safety, honesty and realistic expectations.

Women’s Health & Wellness

Feel Supported Through Every Stage of Life

Changes in hormones, energy, sleep, mood, body composition and sexual health can affect how you feel each day. Dina provides respectful, evidence-informed care that begins with listening to your concerns and understanding your health history, symptoms and goals.

Following a comprehensive assessment, your personalized plan may include education, lifestyle strategies, appropriate investigations, medication or referral when needed.

01

Perimenopause and Menopause Care

Personalized assessment and management for concerns such as hot flashes, night sweats, sleep changes, mood symptoms, brain fog, vaginal dryness, sexual-health changes and changes in body composition.

Hormone Replacement Therapy

Medical Weight Management

Philosophy

You Do Not Need to Know Which Treatment You Need

You can begin with what you see, what you feel or what you would like to improve. During your consultation, Dina will listen to your concerns, assess the relevant anatomy and explain which options may—or may not—be appropriate for you.

You will receive clear information about expected outcomes, treatment limitations, possible risks, recovery and recommended follow-up. There is no pressure to proceed and no one-size-fits-all plan.

About Dina

Meet Dina Wang, MN-NP, NP(F), CAMP

Dina Wang is a Family Nurse Practitioner, Certified Aesthetic Medicine Practitioner (CAMP), advanced aesthetic injector and clinical educator. She earned her Master of Nursing–Nurse Practitioner degree from the University of British Columbia and brings a strong background in nursing, procedural care, women’s health, wellness and medical aesthetics.

With more than 10 years of aesthetic-injection experience, Dina has treated thousands of patients and has extensive experience teaching healthcare professionals. She serves on the nursing faculty of the Canadian Board of Aesthetic Medicine (CBAM) and is an injectable trainer for Clarion Medical Technologies in British Columbia. She provides education in neuromodulators, hyaluronic acid fillers and other advanced injectable treatments. Her clinical approach combines detailed anatomical knowledge, technical precision and an artistic understanding of facial balance.

Dina believes that excellent care should feel personal, safe and honest. Her aim is to help each patient look refreshed, feel supported and make informed decisions about their aesthetic and health goals.

Professional Injectable Training

Clinical Education for Medical Professionals

Natural Beauty Medical Clinic offers comprehensive, safety-focused injectable training for qualified healthcare professionals who want to build practical knowledge, sound clinical judgment and confident technique.

Training combines structured theory with hands-on learning in a small-group environment. Programs emphasize patient assessment, facial anatomy, treatment planning, injection technique, complication prevention, emergency preparedness, documentation and follow-up care.
