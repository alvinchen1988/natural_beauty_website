Professional Injectable Training

Practical Education Grounded in Anatomy and Patient Safety

Natural Beauty Medical Clinic offers injectable education for qualified healthcare professionals seeking structured theory, hands-on learning and stronger clinical judgment.

Dina brings 10 years of injecting experience and extensive experience teaching healthcare professionals. She serves on the nursing faculty of the Canadian Board of Aesthetic Medicine and is an injectable trainer for Clarion Medical Technologies in British Columbia.
