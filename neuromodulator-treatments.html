Medical Aesthetics

Neuromodulator Treatments

Soften Expression Lines While Preserving Natural Movement

Neuromodulators are prescription injectable medications that temporarily reduce activity in selected muscles. They may soften dynamic lines caused by repeated facial expression while maintaining a natural and expressive appearance when treatment is appropriately planned.

Available products may include Botox, Dysport, Xeomin or Nuceiva. Product selection, dosing and placement are individualized. Units are not interchangeable between brands.

Other Selected Uses

Following assessment, neuromodulators may also be considered for concerns such as jaw clenching or prominent masseter muscles and excessive sweating. Suitability, expected benefit and referral needs depend on the concern and the patient’s medical history.

Consultation and Treatment

Dina assesses facial movement at rest and during expression, reviews previous treatment response and discusses the degree of movement you would like to retain. The treatment itself usually involves a series of small injections using a fine needle.

Medical Aesthetics

Results and Follow Up

The effect develops gradually rather than immediately. Timing, strength and duration vary by product, treatment area, dose and individual response. A follow-up may be recommended after the initial effect has developed. Additional treatment is not automatically required and will depend on the assessment.

Medical Aesthetics

Possible Side Effects and Risks

Temporary redness, tenderness, swelling, bruising or headache can occur. Less common effects depend on the area treated and may include asymmetry, unwanted weakness, brow or eyelid heaviness, smile changes or difficulty with nearby muscle function. Rare systemic effects require urgent medical attention. All material risks and alternatives are reviewed during informed consent.

Aftercare

You will receive individualized aftercare instructions. Contact the clinic if you develop unexpected weakness, vision changes, difficulty speaking or swallowing, breathing concerns, or another urgent symptom.

Neuromodulator FAQ

Will I look frozen?+

The treatment plan can be adjusted to your anatomy and desired level of movement. The aim is controlled softening rather than the same result for everyone.

How often is treatment needed?+

Duration varies. Reassessment is recommended when movement and lines begin to return rather than following a fixed schedule for every patient.

Can neuromodulators replace filler?+

No. Neuromodulators affect muscle activity, while fillers support volume and contour. The appropriate option depends on the underlying concern.
