General Questions

Will I be pressured to have treatment?+

No. A consultation may result in a treatment plan, a recommendation to wait, or advice that another provider or service would be more appropriate.

Are consultations required?+

Assessment is required before medical treatment. Some consultations and treatments may occur during the same visit when appropriate, while others require separate planning.

How should I prepare for an injectable appointment?+

Provide a complete medication, allergy and health history. Tell the clinic about pregnancy, breastfeeding, recent illness, dental work, vaccinations, procedures, previous injectable reactions or upcoming events. Do not stop prescribed medication unless advised by the prescribing clinician.

Can I have treatment before an important event?+

Bruising, swelling and delayed reactions can occur. Book with adequate recovery time and avoid scheduling a first-time treatment immediately before a major event or travel.

What if I have a concern after treatment?+

Follow the aftercare instructions and contact the clinic using the provided clinical contact method. Severe pain, skin colour change, vision symptoms, breathing problems or neurological symptoms require urgent assessment.

Women’s Health Questions

Can I book only to discuss symptoms?+

Yes. A consultation can focus on assessment and education. Treatment is not prescribed until it is clinically appropriate and informed consent is complete.

Does everyone in menopause need hormone therapy?+

No. Treatment depends on symptoms, preferences, health risks and clinical indication.

Do you prescribe weight-loss medication to everyone who asks?+

No. Medication requires assessment, clinical eligibility and ongoing monitoring.

Training Questions

Who can attend injectable training?+

Eligibility depends on professional designation, regulatory scope, prescribing or ordering arrangements, course level and provincial requirements.

Does a course certificate mean I am independently competent?+

No. Competence develops through appropriate foundational education, supervised practice, ongoing mentorship and compliance with professional standards.

Are live models included?+

Availability and eligibility vary by program. Details are confirmed before registration.
