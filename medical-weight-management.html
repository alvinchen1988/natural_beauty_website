Medical Weight Management

Support That Goes Beyond a Number on the Scale

Weight is influenced by biology, medications, sleep, stress, hormones, environment, health conditions and daily habits. Medical weight management focuses on health, function and sustainable change rather than shame or unrealistic short-term promises.

Weight Management FAQ

Do you guarantee a specific amount of weight loss?+

No. Response varies and no ethical medical program can guarantee a specific result.

Will I qualify for medication?+

Eligibility depends on clinical criteria, health history, contraindications and shared decision-making.

What happens if I regain weight?+

Weight regain can occur because obesity is a chronic, relapsing condition. The plan can be reviewed without blame and adjusted according to current needs.
