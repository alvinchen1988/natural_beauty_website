Medical Aesthetics

Belkyra

Injectable Treatment for Fullness Beneath the Chin

Belkyra contains deoxycholic acid and is used in appropriate adults to reduce submental fat beneath the chin. It works by disrupting fat cells in the treated area, after which the body gradually clears cellular debris.

Belkyra treats localized fat, not loose skin or every cause of neck fullness. Assessment is important because anatomy, skin quality, salivary glands, muscle structure and other factors can affect the appearance of the area.

Medical Aesthetics

Who May Be a Candidate

Belkyra may be considered for adults with a suitable amount and distribution of submental fat who understand that a series of treatments may be required. Patients with significant skin laxity or another cause of neck fullness may need a different approach.

Medical Aesthetics

Treatment and Recovery

After the area is assessed and marked, a series of injections is placed in a planned pattern. Swelling is expected and can be noticeable, particularly after early sessions. Bruising, tenderness, numbness, firmness and temporary lumps may also occur.

NBTreatment and Recovery image placeholder

Medical Aesthetics

Important Risks

Potential complications include prolonged swelling, uneven contour, difficulty swallowing, skin injury, infection and temporary or persistent weakness of the marginal mandibular nerve, which may affect the smile. All relevant risks and alternatives are reviewed before treatment.

Belkyra FAQ

How many treatments will I need?+

The number varies according to anatomy, response and goals. A series may be required.

Does Belkyra tighten skin?+

It is intended to reduce selected submental fat. It is not a substitute for surgical skin tightening.

Is the swelling significant?+

Swelling is common and may be prominent. Recovery should be planned around work and social commitments.
