About Dina

Advanced Clinical Care with a Personal Approach

Dina Wang is a Family Nurse Practitioner, Certified Aesthetic Medicine Practitioner, advanced aesthetic injector and clinical educator. She earned her Master of Nursing–Nurse Practitioner degree from the University of British Columbia and brings extensive experience in nursing, procedural care, women’s health, wellness and medical aesthetics.

With more than 10 years of aesthetic-injection experience, Dina has treated thousands of patients and has extensive experience teaching healthcare professionals. She serves on the nursing faculty of the Canadian Board of Aesthetic Medicine and is an injectable trainer for Clarion Medical Technologies in British Columbia. Her education work includes neuromodulators, hyaluronic acid fillers, biostimulatory injectables, anatomy, patient assessment, injection technique and complication prevention.

Dina’s Philosophy

Dina believes aesthetic care should enhance a person’s natural features rather than create a standardized look. She considers anatomy, proportion, facial movement, skin quality, personal style and long-term goals before recommending treatment.

Her broader Nurse Practitioner practice follows the same principle. Women seeking support for perimenopause, menopause, weight management or wellness receive an individualized assessment, clear education and a plan based on their health history, symptoms, preferences and clinical needs.
